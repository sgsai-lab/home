WHY WE’RE HERE

AI should make more possible—and keep people in control.

We believe the best technology is useful, understandable, and designed to help people do meaningful work.

Our vision

A future where capable AI helps people and organizations focus their time, creativity, and judgment on what matters.

Our mission

We explore and build thoughtful AI products, and help teams find practical, responsible ways to apply the technology.

Why we do this

AI can create new possibilities, but only when it fits real needs. We want to make the path from an interesting idea to a useful tool clearer and more human.

What we won’t do

We won’t add AI just to follow a trend, make claims we cannot substantiate, or treat people’s data and decisions as an afterthought. We won’t promise outcomes before understanding the work.

“Technology should feel like a door opening, not a world getting more complicated.”

THE PRINCIPLES WE RETURN TO

Our values, in practice

  • Useful over impressiveStart with a real need; choose the simplest approach that can address it.
  • People stay in the pictureDesign around human context, agency, accessibility, and review.
  • Honesty earns trustBe clear about what a system can do, what is uncertain, and where it needs oversight.
  • Privacy by careCollect only what is needed, explain why, and protect it through the full lifecycle.
  • Progress through learningTest assumptions early, listen carefully, and improve based on evidence.
  • Outcomes over outputMeasure success by whether the work helps—not by how much technology was shipped.
