PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Notice

How we handle, protect, and respect your personal data.

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026

SGS AI Technology respects your privacy. This notice outlines how we handle the personal data you provide when interacting with our website. Our commitment is to operate transparently and keep your data secure.

Data Collection via Contact Forms

When you submit an inquiry through our contact form, we collect the following personal information to assist you:

  • Name: To identify who we are communicating with and personalize our response.
  • Email Address: To respond directly to your project inquiry.
  • Phone Number (Optional): To contact you via telephone if requested.
  • Message Content: To understand your business needs and project requirements.

Why We Collect This Data

Your data is collected strictly for the purpose of communicating with you, answering your questions, and facilitating potential business engagements. We do not use this data for automated marketing or sell it to third parties under any circumstances.

Data Retention Period

We believe in strict data minimization. We retain your contact information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose of your inquiry. If your inquiry does not lead to a formal business engagement, your data will be permanently deleted from our communication systems within 90 days.

Analytics and Tracking (Cookieless)

We count page views on our public pages with our own first-party service. It records only the page address and the time, uses no cookies, stores no IP address or device identifier, and does not track you across other websites. No third-party analytics or advertising scripts run on this site.

Accounts and Client Workspaces

When your organisation runs a project with SGS AI Technology, its staff may be invited to a private workspace. Workspaces are for client staff only; we do not create accounts for students or for our clients' own customers.

For account holders we store your name, email address, sign-in method, your role in each workspace, and security records such as active sessions (device type and browser), and a one-way hash of your password if you set one. Two-factor secrets are encrypted. Workspaces hold project details, the apps and services we deliver, and files you or we upload, such as logos, cover images and project deliverables.

This information is stored by Google Cloud (Firestore and Cloud Storage) on our behalf. Files are private and are shared only through links that expire after one hour. When you open an app from your workspace, we record which app was opened and from which device type, to understand usage; these records are deleted after 180 days. A change log of workspace administration (for example, who invited whom) is kept for 90 days.

Signing in sets one strictly necessary cookie that keeps you signed in; it is not used for tracking. The workspace may keep a copy of your app list on your device so it works offline; signing out removes it. You can delete your account at any time from your account page, which removes your personal details and signs you out everywhere.
