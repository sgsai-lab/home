Workspace

Report filters

Date filters apply to activity, workspace profit/loss and invoice/cost registers. Receivables, payables, contract balances, project risks and approvals are current, not historical as-of balances. Archived projects are included unless filtered; deleted projects are excluded.

Organization overview

A project-finance management view, not a balance sheet, statutory profit and loss or bank reconciliation. Platform administrators can report across every workspace, but changing finance records still requires owner and issuer permissions.

Project health

    Monthly activity

    Workspace profit and loss

    Selected projects grouped by customer workspace. Profit/loss estimate = issued invoice net value less credits, approved supplier bills and approved project expenses in the period. Revenue excludes GST; costs include tax. Draft, rejected and void documents do not contribute. This is a live management estimate, not statutory profit and loss; non-project overheads are not included.

    Multi-project portfolio

    Revenue is current non-void invoice net value by issue date, less credits dated in the period. Costs are currently approved, non-void bills and expenses by incurred date, including tax. Margin is an estimate, not tax profit.

    All customer invoices

    Invoices across the selected customer workspaces/projects, filtered by issue date. Includes paid, unpaid, draft, rejected and void invoices, with their current approval and settlement states. Only issued, non-void invoices contribute to revenue. Deals without an invoice are not fabricated or charged automatically. Open a record using existing billing permissions; approval and legal issuance checks still apply.

    All supplier bills and expenses

    Bills and expenses incurred in the selected period, including fully paid, draft, rejected and void records. Only approved, non-void costs contribute to profit/loss or current payables. These are internal costs, not automatic customer invoice charges; do not record the same cost as both a bill and an expense.

    Receivables and overdue invoices

    Current outstanding issued invoices from all dates, after recorded allocations and credits.

    Payables and expenses

    Current approved, non-void unpaid costs from all dates. Expenses without a due date are aged from the incurred date. Record the same cost only once, as a bill or expense.

    Cost categories

    Approved tax-inclusive costs incurred in the selected activity period. Rejected, draft and void costs do not count.

    Cash received and paid

    Manual customer receipts, cost payments, refunds and reversals on their own recorded dates. Reversals remain visible even when the original transaction falls outside the period. This is not a bank feed or a complete accounting cash-flow statement.

    Pending approvals

    Current submitted invoices, bills and expenses across the selected projects, regardless of activity dates. Open a record to review; no bulk approval or permission bypass is provided here.
